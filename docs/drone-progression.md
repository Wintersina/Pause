# Drone progression

Every ship begins with its dock-unlocked dormant slots: stock / upgrade 1 / upgrade 2 / upgrade 3 / final = **1 / 1 / 1 / 2 / 3**. An AIMED core is released above the camera as soon as flight is playable and drifts into view. AIMED chooses a target when firing; its bullets keep that heading afterward. STRAIGHT fires forward and SWEEP alternates 25 degrees left/right.

The directional starter core is the only free opening coin. Enemy kills earn the opening modifier sequence TWIN, TWIN, ARC, one per kill, with the shared four-second pickup spacing. Elite-earned coins consume the same opening slots. Time alone never releases a modifier; earned coins wait in the queue when spacing is blocked. After the opening sequence, ordinary enemy drops use the normal chance and cooldown. TWIN pickups activate any remaining dock-unlocked dormant slots, then upgrade existing drones. They never grant locked slots. ARC upgrades the weakest eligible drone.

Modifiers activate dormant slots first, then upgrade the lowest eligible modifier level with a stable slot-order tie break. RAPID affects the whole squad. Subsequent coin drops have a 20% chance to offer three directional cores: collect one and the others disappear. The current preferred mode occupies the centre. Matching cores upgrade the weakest matching drone to core level four (+25% fire rate per level after the first); alternative modes explicitly reprogram a drone. Labels show the recipient and old/new mode before collection. Fully capped matching cores pay dust.

Each boss drops one recruit. It joins with the preferred mode and half the squad's average modifier levels, rounded down. Existing drones keep their builds. The squad caps at twelve; further recruits pay five dust. All run progress resets on a new run, and carries across worlds/loops.

Upgrades highlight their recipient, reset its firing timer, show the actual before/after benefit, and emphasize its next three volleys. Four projectile silhouettes mark accumulated modifier milestones (4, 12, 25 levels), alongside recoil, dual TWIN muzzle flashes and stronger hit/heart-break/kill bursts. Quarter-heart damage uses the same direct math for ordinary enemies, elites and bosses; enemies have no weapon-hit cooldown.

The HUD shows the selected drone's modifier row and a compact mode pip per slot. During a player pause, it expands one row at a time to show every drone's mode/core and collected modifiers in first-catch order, omitting uncollected types; it collapses on resume. Nearby pickups select their recipient. The banked heal icon sits directly under the tail, falling back above the hull only when the complete badge cannot fit at the bottom edge. All modifier and directional coins, including earned drops, are released beyond the camera top.

Extra wings hover closer with a smaller drift range and fold inside camera edges. Heart turn-backs use a staggered cadence, retaining an owed turn until a safe gap is available. Projectile pools reserve 128 drone bullets plus 40 ultimate projectiles; twelve-drone stress checks cover firing, reuse and corner visibility.

Basic enemies retain two hearts. After the opening 20% of flight, authored tier-three hulls gain three hearts from Frost onward or at power 12; tier-four hulls gain four in Ember or at power 24. Loops unlock those tiers across worlds. Health is chosen at spawn; existing enemies never gain health when a coin is collected. Gold/blue plates distinguish these enemies. Scout wings expand from two to three/four members at four/eight active drones, subject to density and pilot caps. Power combines shared RAPID, modifiers normalized to dock-unlocked starting slots, and four points per active drone after the first (cap 60). Enemy spawn rates follow `1 + (power/60)^.75`, reaching ×2; elite gaps follow `1 - .5*(power/60)^.75`, reaching half-length. This responds to opening upgrades sooner than the old exponent 1.2 / +70% curve. Lane reservations, pilot/threat ceilings, one elite at a time, and the first-elite delay remain in force. Rails do not use the power multiplier. Tutorial pressure is unchanged.

Review: `DroneSquadReview.Export` renders dormant/active/mode-choice/recruit/twelve-drone frames on a 480×854 phone and a Flip cover. `HeartQuarterPreview.ExportCombat` renders real quarter-heart hits and killing hits. Automated fixtures supplement these exports; they do not replace human playtesting.

Spending a pause opens five seconds of double enemy/elite kill points, including the arrival kill. The flight clock holds that window while paused; another pause refreshes it without stacking above ×2. Pickups, passive flight points, boss bonuses and escape penalties keep their normal values. Cooldown-only blinks with no pauses do not grant the bonus.

Fusion notices use the actual contributing atom icons with a small combo name below (for example OVERCLOCK), replacing the large recipe/discovery text. Re-triggering replaces the same compact notice; it freezes with the world and clears on death.

The dock badges show current health and starting drone slots on owned berths and ship cards. A previewed colour that crosses a drone threshold shows +1. Printed bay 15 (Turtle) anchors hull pricing at 15,000 dust; ships 03–15 and their colour prices scale proportionally, while ships 01/02 and Paragon prices stay unchanged.
