Palette for ENEMIES (binding): sand-pale armour hue 40-48, saturation <= .33 (never a saturated gold/amber: that is the pickup colour); weathered oxidised copper hue 22-30, S <= .62, V <= .60, matte; dusty-indigo shadow hue 232-248 as shading only; bone #E6DCC0 / sand #D9CBA0 / mid #BFAE80 / ochre #8F8160 ramps; cores and lenses SUN-WHITE #FFF1C8 (hue 44, S .22) with a bone-white hot centre; tiny verdigris (hue 135-155, S <= .32) or glass-shimmer (hue 160-170, S <= .22) accents only. NO accent in hue 30-48 with S > .45 (amber pickup), none at cyan 178, green 82, violet 250-300, NO red (hue 345-15, S > .5), NO pink/magenta (312-326) anywhere (deviation from the plan's pink-core cue, by instruction: the pink cue is NOT used on Desert enemy art), no orange/amber glow (heat = white-hot/pale).
Readability: enemies must pop off the pale-but-dark-base dune tiles: strong 1 px dark-indigo outline, 2 px bone rim light upper-left, dark body value (V .25-.55) with bright accents only on cores, lenses, rims.
