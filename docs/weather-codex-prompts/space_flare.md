WHAT YOU ARE DRAWING: the body of a SOLAR FLARE, the weather of the Space world. A flare is a wall of hot plasma that sweeps across the screen sideways (a top-down 2D shooter view), shoving ships and bullets in the direction it travels. It must look DRAMATIC and DESIGNED, not a gradient: a white-hot shocked leading edge with flame-lick tendrils, directly behind it a dense gold-orange plasma sheet full of looping magnetic arcs and filaments, then thinner magenta and violet ribbons streaming far behind and dissolving, tiny hot sparks thrown ahead of the shock, a few heat-shimmer ripples. Layered plasma arcs, bright core, hot edges, particle sparks. Ground-up painting: hand-placed-looking neon pixel art with 4-6 tone ramps, crisp hard edges on the hot parts, dithered semi-transparency in the airy tail. NO flat gradient fills, NO blurry glow blobs, NO UI, NO text, no background.
Readability: ships and bullets are drawn OVER it, so the tail and sheet stay semi-transparent (mean alpha of everything behind the core at most about 50%), only the thin core is opaque.
Palette: white-hot #FFF7DC, gold #FFC852, orange #FF8028, hot magenta #FF4DC8, violet #9957FF, deep indigo shade #1A1038. No pure red, no green.

CELL LAYOUT (important, the game places it by these numbers): each cell is a 512 x 512 VERTICAL band seen from above. The hot LEADING EDGE runs top to bottom at x = 400 px (78% of the width, wobbling by at most +-14 px). The plasma tail streams BEHIND it to the LEFT, reaching at most x = 40 (variant A) / x = 170 (variant B, narrower). Sparks fly AHEAD of it to the right (x 410-505), fading out. Every cell is VERTICALLY SEAMLESS: the top pixel row and the bottom pixel row must continue into each other (it is tiled up the screen). Content may touch the left/right edges only as a fade to transparent.
