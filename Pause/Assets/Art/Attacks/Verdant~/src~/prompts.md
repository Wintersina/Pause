# Painted source prompts

Built-in image generation with transparent background. The user-provided Verdant boss, Verdant shots, and Space laser kit were style references. Four candidates were generated and inspected in `candidates/candidate_review.png`.

## `candidates/muzzle_organic.png`

> One isolated asymmetrical acid-hose cannon muzzle flare on transparent canvas. White-hot pink core, scalloped rolling magenta plasma, narrow needle thorns and crackling pink arc sparks, subtle dark leaf-green wet-bark ridges and muted amber-brown sap beads embedded only along the edge. Painterly dense hand-pixeled game art, many small varied pixel clusters, six to seven tone ramps, irregular silhouette, high contrast. Pink family and white hot dominate. No hardware, lettering, backdrop, solid rectangular beam, lime green, red, cyan, or violet.

Used for the early windup and late fade.

## `candidates/muzzle_cross.png`

> One isolated explosive pink-white plasma bloom on transparent canvas, like the split second an acid hose ignites. A sharp four-point lens cross pierces a writhing granular rose-magenta corona; dark leaf-green thorn hooks, wet sap droplets, small broken-shard sparks. Fine painterly neon pixel-art clusters with rich tone depth, a white-pink heart, and hot-pink scalloped mid-band. Clear silhouette at 60 screen pixels. No hull, text, backdrop, flat vector shape, lime, red, cyan, or violet.

Selected for the late windup and sustained muzzle loop: its cross stays distinct at game size.

## `candidates/beam_segment.png`

> One long straight vertical acid-plasma hose segment on a transparent portrait canvas. Thin pink-white core with granular rolling brightness; hot rose-magenta turbulent plasma; thorned dark leaf-green wet bark and leaf edges with sap beads; fine crackling pink arcs and subtle magenta fringing. Intricate painterly neon pixel art with hand-placed clusters and many tones. Uninterrupted body without tip, muzzle, impact, lettering, or environment. No lime, red, cyan, or violet.

Selected for the beam body. Its painted texture was wrapped into a seamless 64 x 128 native tile, recolored to the required hostile palette, and animated with traveling pulses.
