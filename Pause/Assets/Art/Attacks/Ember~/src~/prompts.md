# Painted source candidates

Both candidates were made with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-10-10.
`candidate_painted.png` was selected for its volcanic flame and impact forms.
`candidate_alternate.png` was rejected because the vertical form was too uniform.
Both are retained as source material; `build.py` composes and pixel cleans the
selected painting at 64 px native scale.

## Selected candidate

Transparent sprite concept sheet for a premium pixel-art boss laser attack in a
volcanic forge shooter. Paint five separate isolated effects in one image with
generous spacing: a tiny orbiting pink-white charge, a sharp ignition cross, a
dense hot-pink and white nozzle flare, a long vertical flame-laser with irregular
white core / molten orange filaments / magenta licking edge, and an
upward-spraying impact of magma and pink sparks. Intricate hand-detailed pixel
art like a high-end 1990s arcade boss effect, 5 to 7 nuanced value steps per
material, crisp pixel clusters, no solid flat stripe. Pink #FF4FD8 #FF8AE6
#FFE0F8 and white #FFF4D0 must dominate, orange #E0701C #FF9A2E secondary,
plum smoke #2A1030. True transparent background. No words, no hardware, no
visible grid, no red, amber, green or cyan.

## Alternate candidate

Contact sheet of six distinct isolated energy motifs: a gathering brow charge,
white-hot ignition star, turbulent vertical fire-laser, plasma nozzle bloom,
rail impact, and angular spark cluster. Painterly neon pixel art, hot pink and
white dominant, orange and plum secondary, transparent, no hardware or text.
