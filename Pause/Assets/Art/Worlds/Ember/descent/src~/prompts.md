# Ember image-generation sources

Built-in `image_gen` made the six selected painted PNGs in this folder. The source images were generated independently; the approved Frost and Verdant sets guided the art direction and registration. `generate.py` converts them to indexed RGBA game assets with nearest-neighbour resizing, constructs the curved limb and animation frames, and enforces tile seams and player-red separation. The speed streaks are drawn in the generator.
