# Source art prompts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Each planet used a separate
prompt. Shared direction: one complete centered disc on a genuinely transparent
background for a portrait 2D shooter; sharp, high-resolution painterly pixel art
with discrete clusters and dark linework; upper-right baked light, visible ambient
on the left, cyan right rim; surface detail mainly inside the central 86% radius;
deep-space indigo, violet, magenta and cyan; no red, stars, text or watermark.
The sources were selected and then fitted into exact 4x atlas footprints, clipped
to clean ellipses, and reduced to a 128-color palette per planet.
