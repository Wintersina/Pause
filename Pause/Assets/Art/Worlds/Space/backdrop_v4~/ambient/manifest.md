# Space ambient atlas v4

Image-generated source art packed as transparent Unity sprites. The concept render and existing atlases guided the visual style. Existing Space assets were read only.

| Atlas | Size | Grid | Sprites | Pixel scale |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| nebula.png | 2048×2048 | 4×4, 512 px cells | 16 | 2 |
| twinkle.png | 1024×512 | 8×4, 128 px cells | 32 | 2 |
| debris.png | 2048×1024 | 8×4, 256 px cells | 32 | 2 |

JSON rectangles use bottom-origin y. All rectangles are in bounds, pairwise non-overlapping, and centered in their cells with at least 14 px transparent margin. p90 value is HSV V (maximum RGB channel / 255) among pixels with alpha > 8; peak alpha and edge alpha are /255. The edge band is 14 px on each side of a nebula cell. Source art was resized only with nearest-neighbour; technical packing normalization caps nebula opacity at 140/255 and dims ambient RGB to meet the requested background limits.

Nebula frames 12–15 reuse the exact silhouettes and alpha of frames 00–03 with dimmer filaments for two-frame breathing. Twinkle rows are cool white, cyan-blue, orange, and violet-pink; columns are sequential frames.
