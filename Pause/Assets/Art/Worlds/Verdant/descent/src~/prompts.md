# Verdant planetfall painted sources

The six `*_source.png` files were created with built-in `image_gen`, inspected, and selected for this set. `planet_candidate_a.png` is the rejected globe candidate; `planet_source.png` is the selected second globe. `generate.py` registers, scales, color indexes, animates, and tiles these sources with nearest-neighbor sampling. The streak layer and small accents are drawn in the generator.

## Selected source prompts

### `planet_source.png`

Alternate candidate for a 2D shooter Verdant planet pixel-art sprite. Centered complete jungle-industrial globe on fully transparent field. Spherical dark emerald and teal canopy ocean, wide winding glowing lime rivers, copper rusted cities with tall vine-choked towers, magenta and lime spore lights on dark lower-right night side, thin lime atmosphere and softly dithered terminator. A dramatic leaf-shaped incomplete orbital trellis of roots with glowing seed-pod lanterns wraps diagonally across the equator, distinctly different from a heavy metal station ring, readable in silhouette. Expert hand-placed 16-bit pixel art with crisp limited palette, complex believable surface geography, high contrast day-night shading, square pixel clusters. No ice, no blur, no text, no starfield.

### `limb_surface_source.png`

Pixel-art Verdant planet surface material for a 2D shooter planetary approach. Straight top-down oblique orbital view of terrain filling the entire rectangular image edge to edge, no horizon and no globe. Dense emerald and teal jungle canopy in giant uneven masses, winding broad lime bioluminescent rivers, corroded copper industrial districts with towers and pipe trellises overgrown by vines, tiny magenta-pink spore lights. Canopy brighter lime-green near top, darker teal and blue-green toward bottom. Yellow-green pollen haze and translucent spore drifts. Intricate handcrafted 16-bit game pixel art with sharp square pixel clusters and dark outlines; no blur, no ice, no empty space, no text.

### `cloud_source.png`

Repeating bright Verdant spore cloud deck texture. Top-down orbital view filled with overlapping billowing pollen-cloud tops and cumulus lobes, warm yellow-lime crests, pale green-white highlights, turquoise and deep teal shadows, suspended spore sparkles, occasional dark green gaps. Even texture coverage without a focal subject; cloud shapes cross all four borders. Handcrafted pixel art with crisp square clusters and limited palette; no blur, horizon, globe, or text.

### `cloud_dark_source.png`

Repeating dark Verdant spore cloud deck texture. Top-down view filled with shadowy emerald, deep teal, and muted violet pollen-cloud billows, curling tendrils, wide organic openings to dark jungle, selectively lit yellow-green crests and tiny magenta-pink spores. Even coverage with shapes crossing all four borders. Hand-authored 16-bit pixel art, crisp clusters, limited palette; no blur, horizon, globe, or text.

### `entry_source.png`

Transparent-background 2D atmospheric-entry plasma sprite source in a tall portrait composition. Deliberately empty transparent oval ship opening centered in the upper third. Lime-gold and emerald plasma curls around this hole and forms a long turbulent S-shaped wake: white-hot core, acid yellow-green middle flames, emerald outer wisps, magenta-pink sparks, seed spores, sharp leaf particles. Ragged asymmetric silhouette, crisp stepped pixel clusters, limited palette; no blur, background, ship, or text.

### `breakthrough_source.png`

Transparent-background cloud-break burst sprite source. Centered shock explosion viewed from above: dense lime-white flash disc and expanding broken shells of yellow-green pollen puffs, emerald vapor crescents, spore spray, leaf shards, a few magenta-pink sparks. Irregular silhouette and radial rays with turquoise teal shadow in cloud undersides. Handcrafted pixel art, crisp square clusters, limited luminous palette; no blur, empty thin ring, or text.

## Selection note

The first globe candidate had a strong foreground root arc but a less spherical globe. The selected second candidate had a clearer orbital leaf silhouette, more legible night side, and better curved shading. Both source candidates are retained here. The generated limb terrain deliberately had no horizon; the curved limb and lime atmosphere are registered to Frost's exact horizon curve in `generate.py`.
