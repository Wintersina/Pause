# Storm limb image generation prompts

Built-in `image_gen` edit mode, transparent background. The supplied `old_limb.png` was the edit target for A and B. Candidate C used B as its edit target and the old limb as a palette and mood reference. B was selected for `build.py`.

## Candidate A

Use case: style-transfer. Asset: 2D game planet limb, ultra-wide 2:1 landscape, transparent space above. Edit target: the attached Storm limb. Preserve its slate and ink-blue storm palette, desaturated bronze-grey haze, dense richly textured hand-placed-looking pixel art, two cyclone eyes, cauliflower thunderheads, tiny floating refinery platforms and balloons, sparse pale yellow-white lightning, and pale blue-white atmosphere rim. Repaint the entire planetary surface with truly spherical grazing-angle perspective. Keep the exact outer horizon arc: apex at x=50%, y=35% of canvas; horizon descends to y=70% at both side edges. Every storm stratum must be a concentric arc parallel to that horizon, curving DOWN at both sides; absolutely no straight horizontal cloud bands. Near the rim, details become tiny, flattened, low contrast, packed into thin layers; toward the bottom, they expand into large tall cloud formations and broad storm spirals. Cyclone eyes are thin ellipses in the distance and broad visible spirals near the viewer. Floating platforms and balloons near the horizon are much smaller. Deep atmospheric haze and limb darkening beneath a slightly thicker layered blue-white rim. Lighting from upper left. Match the existing composition and dramatic mood. Solid transparent upper area, no stars, no sky backdrop, no text, no magenta, lime, cyan fields, red, or orange glow. Rich tonal ramps, crisp pixel texture, no vector shapes, no smooth airbrush, no posterisation.

## Candidate B — selected

Use case: style-transfer. Edit the supplied Storm planet limb for a phone game's descent scene. Keep the original dramatic dark slate-blue and ink-blue pixel-art storm world, desaturated bronze-grey bands, pale yellow-white lightning, floating black refinery balloon platforms, two spiral cyclone eyes, cauliflower thunderheads, rich textured tonal ramps. The planet is a HUGE ROUND SPHERE seen at a very shallow grazing angle, not a flat painting. Exact composition: transparent upper 35%; smooth circular horizon peaks in centre at 35% image height and drops to 70% at both edges; lower area fully planetary atmosphere. Make perspective much stronger: from the horizon down the first quarter of the planet, storm bands are extremely thin and close packed, cloud banks are low narrow ridges, spiral eyes nearly collapsed to narrow ellipses and floating structures tiny. Progressively towards bottom, curved bands open into broad concentric arcs and the thunderheads swell into towering masses with large spiral eyes. All bands and cloud ridges bend downward toward both edges in concentric arcs parallel to the horizon, with clear spherical topology. Keep atmospheric blue-white rim thick and layered, hazy and darkened just beneath it; light from upper left. Crisp fine detailed pixel painting, no horizontal straight bands, no cyan patches, no magenta, lime, red, orange glow, no text, no stars.
