# Storm ground sets v3 (night side) and v4 (gust-front shelf arcs) -- Codex briefs

v1 (banded diagonal deck, amber shear haze, curl scars) and v2 (cauliflower thunderhead towers with amber crests) are
installed and both are DAY sets, so v3 is the NIGHT SIDE (`variantNight`) and v4 is a third day place. The earlier
"eye of the storm" ground set was removed by the user: do NOT paint a cyclone eye / single huge spiral.
Each set = two 1254x1254 paintings (a, b). build_tiles_v34.py stitches a over b into a 1024x2048 tile.

Shared constraints (pasted into every prompt): hi-res neon pixel art, crisp hand-placed pixel clusters, 5-8 tone ramps,
no blur, no airbrush, no photo, no 3D; orthographic top-down view from just under the ceiling of a gas-giant atmosphere,
cloud fills every edge, evenly dense edge to edge, NO horizon/sky/planet/stars/space/water/ocean/waves/foam/ships/
structures/UI/text/border/vignette; NO outlines or white borders; palette = indigo and graphite cloud, teal-slate
shadow, amber-peach crest rims, white-hot blue-edged lightning; FORBIDDEN pink, magenta, red, green, brown mud.
Central 50% of the width calmer and lower contrast (bullets and enemies read there), outer 25% each side busiest.
