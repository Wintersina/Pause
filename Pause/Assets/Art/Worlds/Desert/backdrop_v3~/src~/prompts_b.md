# Desert backdrop run B — built-in image generation prompts

The five selected painted source sheets were generated with the built-in image tool as isolated transparent sprite contact sheets. `weather_source_b.png` was a second weather candidate; its opaque gradient background made it less suitable, so `weather_source_a.png` was selected. The actual final atlas pixels are assembled by `build_b.py` from the selected source sheets.

## Shared direction

Production sprite-source sheet for Pause, a phone-portrait vertical 2D shooter. Atmosphere-level high-oblique bird's-eye view over a dead strip-mined Desert planet. Rugged cyberpunk and restrained steampunk industrial pixel art: dense sharp hand-placed-looking clusters, dark selective outlines, 4–6 textured tone bands, scratches and rivets, restrained rim light. Dark base, pale desaturated bone-gold sand, dusty indigo shadows, matte oxidised copper, trace muted patina, tiny pale sun-white lamps. Each distinct subject isolated in its own transparent cell with generous clear space, no drawn grid or text. No worms, people, space imagery, UI, red/pink/violet, orange/amber glow, saturated cyan/green or smooth blur.

## Landmark sheet (`landmarks_source_a.png`)

4×4: two half-buried refinery hulls; two ribcage gantry towers; drill-head tower; two dead solar-array skeleton fields; two crawler caravans; radio mast; collapsed dome cluster; derelict bucket-wheel excavator; sunken cargo crawler; fused-glass crater rim; copper cliff arch; pump-well station. Keep each silhouette asymmetric, detailed and grounded with only a tiny broken sand drift at its contact points.

## Pipe sheet (`pipes_source_a.png`)

4×4: two straights; two bends; tee; cross; two multi-valve manifolds; two pipe bridges on dark lattice trestles; pump house; two burst seam leaks with visible emitter bases and pale white steam; half-buried pipe; two pipe tangles. Weathered brass/copper, dark seams, sand at feet; obvious connector geometry.

## Ground-feature sheet (`features_source_a.png`)

4×4: two wind-polished rock fins; two sand drifts against derelict walls; two glass fulgurite clusters; two dry wells; two bleached machine-rib groups; two heat vents with pale-white heat only; crack scar; narrow dune ridge with indigo lee; scattered wreckage; lone rusted crawler. Small discrete ground features, no broad backdrop tiles.

## Weather sheet (`weather_source_a.png`)

4×4: four large thin dust-ceiling cloud banks; four torn high wisps; low veils; directional wind streaks; diffuse dust palls; loose sand-grain particles. Continue the desaturated dusty bone-grey and indigo-brown planetfall cloud deck. Semi-transparent, low contrast, no flat edge cuts. The final 16 cells use four banks, four wisps, three mists, two gusts, two palls and one particle group.

## Site sheet (`sites_source_a.png`)

4×3: closed/open pairs with shared footprint for a mechanical worm-burrow hatch (no worm visible), crawler bay, fused-glass pad, buried silo and ruined dock; then marker lights off/on. Open states have a dark center emergence point. Tiny pale-white lamps; no emissive copper.

## Rejected weather variation (`weather_source_b.png`)

The alternate asked for a 4×4 transparent sheet with stronger dust-deck continuity and square-pixel feathering. It produced attractive cloud shapes but an opaque gradient filling the whole sheet, so it was retained only as a candidate record.
