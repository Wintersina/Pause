Palette strictly dusty desaturated bone gold and matte dark copper with faint indigo shadows; no bright orange, no red, no pink, no violet, no cyan, no green, no space imagery. Transparent background, no text, no UI, no ground rectangle. Full silhouette comfortably inside square canvas with generous margin. Light from top left.
