# Ridge fix: built-in image generation

Four transparent candidates were generated with the built-in image generation tool. `ridge_candidate_d.png` supplies the selected low mound; `ridge_candidate_b.png` supplies the dust wake. Candidate A's triangular wake and candidate C's leaf-like central crest were rejected. The final sheets use nearest-neighbour resampling and pixel cleaning in `ridge_fix.py`.

## Candidate A

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent painted key frame source for a pixel-art 2D vertical shooter background. ONE low rolling mound of dune sand caused by a giant sand worm moving just beneath the surface. Camera looks obliquely downward from atmosphere level. The WORM MUST NOT BE VISIBLE. Composition: broad horizontally rounded oval hump near the upper third of a square frame, about 60% of image width and only 30-40% of image height; a thin curving crest across its top, light upper-left slope and dusty indigo lee flank, with little sliding sand, fine irregular cracks, tiny hopping pebbles. Beneath it, a sparse low dust wake streams down-screen to a bottom-center ground-contact point, fading to transparent. The entire visual is a shallow piece of dune surface, never upright: NO feather, leaf, tornado, spikes, plume, horn, tail, exposed creature, or vertical spine. Crisp intricate hand-placed-looking pixel art with stepped edges and local tonal ramps, restrained pale desaturated bone-gold sand on a dark base, narrow ivory crest highlights, dusty indigo shadows. Matte, quiet background object. Genuine transparent background around the shape; no rectangle or ground tile. No orange or amber glow, no red, pink, magenta, violet, saturated cyan/green, space imagery, text, UI, or watermark.

## Candidate B

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a single transparent key painting for a 2D top-down oblique desert game animation. Depict an enormous sand worm ONLY by the displaced dune surface; no body, no mouth, no creature anatomy. The silhouette must be a LOW, WIDE, SOFTLY ROUNDED HORIZONTAL DUNE HUMP, like a compressed oval hill, seen from above at an oblique angle. Broad hump sits across the upper middle and occupies roughly 60% of square frame width, and 30% of frame height. A narrow winding ridge crest runs across the breadth, pale on its sunward side; one broad dusty indigo shadow flank on the right. Fine wind ripple marks, shallow hairline cracks, sand grains sliding off both shallow slopes, scattered tiny pebbles. Below the broad hump is only a very narrow, faint, short trail of disturbed sand tapering toward the bottom center. Emphasize the mound, not the wake. No upright structure. No leaf, feather, tornado, pointed apex, spikes, plume, or vertical central rib. Rich detailed crisp pixel-painted surface and stair-step edges, subdued bone-gold with several desaturated tones on a dark base, thin bright bone crest. Keep paint isolated on genuine transparent background; no hard tile boundary. Absolutely no red, yellow, orange, amber light, pink, violet, cyan, green, text, UI, or space elements.

## Candidate C

Use case: stylized-concept. Isolated source painting for a tiny pixel art desert game sprite. Paint a single broad and shallow dune swelling, viewed obliquely from high above: a low horizontal oval sand hummock, wider than it is tall, like a rolling underwater wave seen through sand. Fine pale crest line curves across it. Lit desaturated bone-gold upper flank, dark dusty-indigo lower-right flank, tiny wind ripples, shallow hairline cracks, sliding grains and a few hopping pebbles. Its shape occupies the center of a square canvas, with ample transparent margin. Keep its outline softly irregular but rounded, no sharp point or peak. Crucially no wake, tail, leaf, feather, tornado, vertical spike, worm body or anatomy. This is a low mound of ground surface, approximately 2.5 times as wide as tall. Very detailed handmade crisp pixel painting, short material ramps, dark restrained values, only thin pale highlight on the crest. Transparent background. No red, orange, amber glow, yellow, pink, violet, saturated cyan/green, space, UI, text, watermark.

## Candidate D (selected mound)

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: isolated transparent key painting for a small pixel-art desert backdrop animation. A giant underground worm makes a low rolling swell in a dune sea. Seen from a high, oblique overhead camera: one broad, flattened, horizontally stretched mound, as wide as two shallow barchan ripples, no steep peak. The outer silhouette should merge softly into ground dust, irregular and low, not a neat oval cutout. Across its upper third runs a long, lateral, gently scalloped pale bone-gold crest from left to right. Beneath the crest, sand slides down a shallow front flank; the right lower flank falls into dusty indigo lee shadow. Hairline cracks, horizontal wind ripples, small hopping pebbles. Roughly 2.5 times as wide as the mound is tall. The worm itself is fully buried and invisible. No central diagonal seam, no leaf vein, no feather, no pointed wake, no vertical spike, no tail or plume. Handcrafted dense crisp pixel art, short desaturated sand tonal ramps, narrow highlight only on crest, dark base matching weathered dune ground. Whole painted mound inside a square transparent canvas with generous margin. No orange/amber glow, red, magenta, violet, green, cyan, space imagery, text, UI, or watermark.
