# Powers v2 manifest

Generated by `python3 generate.py` (Pillow required). The 30 runtime PNGs are RGBA; the contact sheet is RGB. Palettes use the ship hull colours, with a violet-blue singularity for Saboteur.

Each strip is **4096 × 256 px**: 16 contiguous **256 × 256 px** cells. Frames 0–7 are an eight-frame seamless animated power state or projectile/charge; frames 8–15 are a ship-specific trigger or impact. Cell coordinates are `(256 * frame, 0, 256, 256)`. Transparent pixels surround the art. Intended Unity filter: Point. Shapes use filled shadow/body/highlight tones, pale pixel rims, and stepped glow.
