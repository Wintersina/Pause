# Ember Elite Ship Set

Art-only concept set. These are intentionally not wired into `Resources`, the enemy roster, or player hull systems.

Every final PNG is a transparent `1344 x 192` strip of seven equal `192 x 192` cells. The art is rebuilt from the compact GoldWarden player hull's top-down silhouette, pivot, centered fuselage, swept wings, and paired rear-engine layout, at roughly 20% larger gameplay scale. Ember identity comes from soot-black furnace steel, copper wear, furnace orange and magenta details inside that player-style geometry.

The seven cells are: landed/resting, grounded idle, lift-off, airborne hover, bank-left, bank-right/pursuit, and second-heart damaged-but-active flight. Frames 0–5 contain one connected full ship with no free fragments; frame 6 remains an intact controllable ship with scorched/cracked armor and attached smoke rather than destruction debris. Final silhouettes stay inset by at least 16px in each cell so neighboring art cannot bleed into playback.

These are elite player-derived forgecraft, not enemy recolors and not player-hull edits.
