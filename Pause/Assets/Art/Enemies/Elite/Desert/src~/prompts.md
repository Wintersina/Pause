# Desert elite image-generation prompts

Built-in image-generation tool, `transparent_background=true`, ten distinct calls: A and B for each ship. The images in this folder are the unselected and selected raw candidates; `<name>_master.png` records each selected input. The output `*_concept.png` files are palette-cleaned selected paintings.

## Candidate A shared prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one isolated top-down 2D pixel-art enemy spacecraft painting for a portrait phone shooter. Make exactly ONE whole ship in a square composition, nose pointing straight UP and rear engine nozzles down, perfectly centered, generous empty transparent margins. Crisp detailed pixel art, dark 1-pixel selective outlines, 4-6 tone textured ramps, scuffs, rivets, rim lighting, thousands of subtle color variations; hard pixels, no blurry rendering. Setting language: a dead, sun-bleached strip-mined desert planet at atmosphere level. Material: matte oxidised copper and dark ochre armour, sand-scoured desaturated pale bone-gold edges, dusty indigo recesses. Tiny sun-white #FFF1C8 lamps and engine apertures only. Warm sand hue 40-48 saturation <= .33; matte copper hue 22-30 <= .62 saturation. No background, no ground, no pedestal, no text, no UI, no labels, no other ships, no weapons being fired. No orange or amber glow, red, pink, violet, saturated cyan/teal/mint/green, space imagery. Transparent background with true alpha.

## Candidate B shared prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: isolated enemy ship game art. Exactly ONE ship, full top-down orthographic view, nose straight up, centered with generous transparent margins. Detailed crisp hand-crafted pixel art, 1 px dark outline, varied 4-6 step painterly ramps, scratches, rivets and plate seams. Material: MATTE dark oxidised copper and desaturated bone-gold sand-worn edges, dusty indigo recesses, pale silver glass; very tiny SUN-WHITE lights. Dry industrial desert. TRUE transparent alpha, absolutely no backdrop, shadow ground, landscape, text or UI. No red, orange glow, amber glow, pink, violet, saturated cyan/teal/green. No soft blur. Preserve strong readable silhouette.
