# Frost elite concept prompt record

All eight paintings were generated as single isolated top-down ships on transparent backgrounds. Every prompt required an upward nose, downward engines, symmetric idle pose, fully visible craft, rugged rime steel, chipped glacier ice, cyan coolant, sparse brass, detailed neon pixel-art treatment, selective dark outline, no red, no text, no scenery, and no perspective angle.

| Ship | First candidate (`*_master.png`) | Alternate (`*_alt.png`) | Selected |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Floe Harrower | Broad flat ice-barge with two splayed ski pontoons, central cutter fan, four rear slab chutes. | Darker, more rectangular ice barge with side runners, forward fan and four rear chutes. | First. The larger open pontoon gap makes its outline clearest. |
| Cryo Siren | Long needle spine with large luminous circular coolant dish and trailing hoses. | Darker, thinner dish ship with two conspicuous hose loops. | First. The large cyan dish remains readable after reduction. |
| Glacier Tender | Boxy tug with four deck pods, mast beacon, side winches and heavy paired engines. | Dark steel tug with four larger dome pods, clear cable reels and armored rear engines. | Alternate. Pods and industrial winches read better. |
| Whiteout Sentinel | Pale three-plate ice wedge over a dark rear hull with cyan eye slit. | Three distinct ice plates on a broader dark steel tank wedge with one cyan slit. | Alternate. The steel-to-ice contrast is stronger at sprite size. |

The production script uses only the selected image per ship for all seven cells. It never asks image generation to repaint a banked or damaged hull, which keeps the ship identity and scale stable frame to frame. The source masters and alternates here are the inspection candidates; the four public `_concept.png` files are copies of the selected paintings.
