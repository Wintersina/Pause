Generate ONE isolated Verdant elite ship, Timber Hauler, centered with roomy transparent padding, nose pointing exactly upward, thrusters at bottom. This is a wide, squat, heavy industrial log barge, about 1.2 times as wide as tall; unmistakable strapped tree trunks in a recessed back cradle, twin mechanical grabber arms hinged at the sides and a prominent transverse roller drum at the front. Highly detailed painterly pixel art with many nuanced shades, crisp pixel edges, 1-pixel selective dark outlines, pale 2-pixel rim highlights on upper-left edges, scratches, rivets, grain and facets. Dark green big surfaces with bright emerald focal points so it reads on a dark green jungle floor. Orthographic top-down 2D game sprite, cohesive solid silhouette, no perspective tilt, no floor, no drop shadow, no labels, no UI, no other ships. True transparent alpha. Avoid red and red-orange, cyan, violet, bright lime, broad yellow, broad orange. Do not draw shots or hearts. Output a single ship master key frame, no sprite sheet.
