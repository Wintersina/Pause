# Storm Rod Mine painted source

Built-in image generation, transparent RGBA, with `rail_mines_neon_tide.png` as a style and layout reference only. Two generated atlas candidates are preserved as `candidate_a.png` and `candidate_b.png`; A was selected for its dark slate hull and clearer induction coil. The painted candidates are source art, not final cells.

## Selected candidate prompt

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: pixel art game sprite sheet source. Use the supplied Tide rail mine ONLY as a composition and craft reference, redraw a distinctly new Storm-world lightning-rod mine. Create FOUR equally spaced separate horizontal poses on a fully transparent canvas: Dormant, Waking, Charging, Burst, no borders or labels. Same detailed rugged 2D pixel art seen in reference: clearly pixelated hard stepped edges, dense hand painted 4-6 tone ramps per material, 1-pixel dark outlines, numerous bolts, scratches, layered steel. Each pose: identical solid dark steel left wall clamp block at the same relative position; its left edge about 128 final pixels left of sphere center; sphere body center same y and nearly same x across panels. Design a slate/graphite armored spherical lightning-rod with a coil-wrapped white core and two short steel rod spikes at top and bottom. Top-left pale cold rim light 2 px. Dormant core storm white #F2F6FF but contained. Waking core brighter and tiny white-blue arcs crawling coil. Charging rod spikes extend and a growing translucent white-blue storm-white halo, feathered to fully transparent well inside pose bounds. Burst is a striking radial starburst of jagged lightning rays in storm white #F2F6FF edged pale yellow #FFF3A0, remains inside panel, steel clamp unchanged. Palette tightly restricted: bruised slate, graphite gunmetal, pale hail grey, muted teal grey, dark outlines, cold white-blue arc glow, tiny sodium-white work lights. No red, orange, amber glow, cyan fields, green, violet, magenta, mint, brown-brass hull, text, UI, background, framing, drop shadows. Transparent RGBA. Keep every full pose separate with generous clear margins, all four same body scale and anchor. Aim for approved Tide source sprite craftsmanship but original Storm design.

`build.py` uses nearest-neighbour transforms and reuses the chosen painted hull and clamp across states. It retains painted lightning rays and imposes the exact atlas geometry.
