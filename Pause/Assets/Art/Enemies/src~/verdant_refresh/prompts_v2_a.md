# Verdant refresh: second-pass painted source prompts

The built-in image generator painted a new transparent sheet for each unit. Each sheet has three different complete concepts, not animation frames. The first-pass candidate sheet was supplied only as a shape and role reference. After generation, stray red, magenta, violet, and cyan fringe pixels in the candidate sheets were shifted to desaturated tan; the painted forms remain image-generated. `build.py` selects the middle Briar Dart and the left Spore Lobber. The Spore Lobber source concepts overlap slightly, so the build mirrors the complete painted left half of the chosen concept at the iris seam.
