# Verdant fighter source paintings

Both chosen source sheets were generated with the built-in image-generation tool
on transparent canvases. Each has three full alternative designs, not animation
frames. The selected painting is column 1 (zero based) in each sheet. The
candidate sheets were palette-cleaned to remove stray generated red fringes;
`build.py` crops the selected design and resamples with nearest-neighbour only.

## `verdant_fighter_2_candidates.png` — selected generation

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: painted pixel-art source sheet for a 2D shooter enemy.
Create exactly THREE DIFFERENT COMPLETE concepts of a DRILL WASP spacecraft, side by side in three separate equal columns, on a genuinely transparent canvas. They are three design alternatives, not animation frames.
Each concept is an extremely SLIM vertical DART with a narrow tapered armored fuselage, top-down orthographic and perfectly vertical, nose and helical BRASS DRILL pointing straight DOWN. The dark bronze and olive-brown body is only about one-quarter to one-third as wide as it is tall. Two separate thin TRANSLUCENT LEAF WINGS sprout at mid body left and right, swept back/down like a fast wasp, each wing with visible veins and a dark metal leading spar. Total silhouette is about 110 wide by 130 tall after downscaling. Add ONE tiny lime-white eye gem at upper body, about 4% of ship width. The drill is visibly a short spiral with brass banding, not a plain spike.
Premium dense game pixel art. Stepped crisp edges, 1-pixel selective deep-brown outline, pale upper-left edge rim, 4–6 dark material tones, rivets, scratches, bark grain. The bronze bark-armored hull must read first. A few leaf-green veins, under 10% of body. Dark materials V .25–.55. Symmetric silhouette; no perspective tilt.
Generous transparent separation and margins. No round fuselage, no spherical reactor, no broad manta-wing body, no limb claws, no humanoid or bug face, no scenery, ground, shadow, glow field, UI, letters, labels, or text. NO red, orange, pink, magenta, violet, blue, cyan, or teal anywhere. No slime. One tiny pale lime eye only.
```

## `verdant_fighter_3_candidates.png`

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: source concept sheet for a vertical 2D shooter enemy sprite
Primary request: Create THREE DIFFERENT COMPLETE concepts of the same "Scythe Mantis" melee-feel gunship, side by side on ONE genuinely transparent canvas, evenly separated, each one isolated and fully visible.
Subject: a symmetric mechanical mantis craft viewed TOP-DOWN ORTHOGRAPHIC, facing straight DOWN toward the bottom of the canvas. A dark olive-brown and bronze bark-and-brass armored central hull dominates. Small head at the top with ONE tiny lime-white lens eye, two long curved leaf-scythe blades folded forward/down on left and right sides, narrow spiked segmented abdomen tapering to the bottom, and two short articulated antennae. Three distinct concepts of this exact anatomy, not three animation frames. Clear symmetrical broad mantis silhouette.
Style/medium: premium dense hand-painted pixel-art sprite, crisp stepped edges, selective one-pixel dark outline, two-pixel pale upper-left rim light, distinct 4–6-tone material ramps, legible rivets, small scratches and organic grain. Hard material planes, sophisticated detailed texture, readable dark hull-first silhouette at phone size.
Color palette: dark bronze, muted bark brown, deep olive, tiny leaf-green blade veins and one tiny lime-white eye (#E9FFB8). Lime/light green covers no more than 10% of each body. Hull stays dark, values about .25–.55.
Composition/framing: three complete separate sprites in equal columns, generous empty transparent margins; each roughly 130 wide by 130 high in final use. Upper-left key light.
Constraints: no labels, text, UI, scenery, shadows, ground, or background. No side views or perspective tilt. No red, pink, magenta, violet, blue or cyan. No fluorescent slime smeared across armor, no large bright-green fields, no orange/amber glows. All three whole and uncropped.
```
