# Verdant fighter source paintings

Both source sheets were regenerated with the built-in image-generation tool on
transparent canvases. Each has three full alternative designs, not animation
frames. The selected second-pass paintings are column 0 for Drill Wasp and
column 2 for Scythe Mantis (zero based). Candidate sheets were palette-cleaned
to remove generated red fringes; `build.py` crops the selected design and
resamples with nearest-neighbour only.

## First-pass `verdant_fighter_2_candidates.png` prompt (superseded)

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: painted pixel-art source sheet for a 2D shooter enemy.
Create exactly THREE DIFFERENT COMPLETE concepts of a DRILL WASP spacecraft, side by side in three separate equal columns, on a genuinely transparent canvas. They are three design alternatives, not animation frames.
Each concept is an extremely SLIM vertical DART with a narrow tapered armored fuselage, top-down orthographic and perfectly vertical, nose and helical BRASS DRILL pointing straight DOWN. The dark bronze and olive-brown body is only about one-quarter to one-third as wide as it is tall. Two separate thin TRANSLUCENT LEAF WINGS sprout at mid body left and right, swept back/down like a fast wasp, each wing with visible veins and a dark metal leading spar. Total silhouette is about 110 wide by 130 tall after downscaling. Add ONE tiny lime-white eye gem at upper body, about 4% of ship width. The drill is visibly a short spiral with brass banding, not a plain spike.
Premium dense game pixel art. Stepped crisp edges, 1-pixel selective deep-brown outline, pale upper-left edge rim, 4–6 dark material tones, rivets, scratches, bark grain. The bronze bark-armored hull must read first. A few leaf-green veins, under 10% of body. Dark materials V .25–.55. Symmetric silhouette; no perspective tilt.
Generous transparent separation and margins. No round fuselage, no spherical reactor, no broad manta-wing body, no limb claws, no humanoid or bug face, no scenery, ground, shadow, glow field, UI, letters, labels, or text. NO red, orange, pink, magenta, violet, blue, cyan, or teal anywhere. No slime. One tiny pale lime eye only.
```

## Second pass: warm readable hulls

The new sheets replace the first-pass candidate paintings in place. The selected
concepts are column 0 for Drill Wasp and column 2 for Scythe Mantis.

```text

Make the full wingspan about 85% of its length, so the final sprite can be about 140 x 164 pixels. HULL FIRST. Central armor is PALE AGED BRASS, bone-tan and sun-bleached bark, generous sunlit plate surfaces in #CDB777 #E7D9A0 #B89A5A, medium olive brass #8E7A44, with dark olive-brown only inside seams and recesses. A strong crisp 2-pixel pale ivory upper-left rim #F1F6C8 follows the outside silhouette. Wing membranes are LUMINOUS yellow-lime to leaf-green, with pale veins and some transparency but no large slime fields. One jewel core has lime-white #E9FFB8 center. Premium intricate hand-painted pixel art with crisp stepped pixels and rich fine rivets, scratched plates, wood grain, 4-6 local tones, selective 1 px dark outline; lively and bright enough to read against dark mid-green forest. Each of three designs remains isolated and fully visible, with generous transparent space between columns. No labels, letters, scenery, cast shadows, background, UI or animation frames. No red, pink, magenta, violet, blue, cyan or teal pixels. No orange glow, no amber pickup color, no dark overall muddy body.
```
