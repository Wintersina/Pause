# Verdant refresh painted sources

Built-in image generation made each transparent sheet. Each contains three complete
concepts, side by side. The middle column (index 1) was selected for both units.
`build.py` crops that source, samples it with nearest-neighbour, moves the named
parts for idle, then adds restrained tell and hit marks. These are the exact
generation prompts:

## `verdant_chaser_candidates.png`

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: three candidate painted pixel-art source sprites for a Unity portrait 2D shooter. Create ONE transparent horizontal sheet containing THREE DIFFERENT COMPLETE Briar Dart spaceship concepts, one isolated sprite in each equal-width column, with generous empty transparent space between them. Each is a slim, rigid, bilaterally symmetric arrowhead about twice as tall as wide, TOP-DOWN ORTHOGRAPHIC with the nose/front pointed straight DOWN, no tilt, no perspective. Recognizable anatomy in all three: narrow layered bark-plate and aged olive-bronze/brass armoured central hull; two curled leaf-blade fins (one on each side); hooked thorn-lance nose at bottom; ONE small lime-white lens eye in the central hull; tiny thorn legs. Three different silhouette solutions, NOT animation frames. Hull first: most of each body is dark weathered olive-brown bark and muted brass, material value 0.25-0.55, 4-6 tone richly textured ramps, believable inset seams, rivets, edge scratches, directional grain. Clean 1-pixel dark selective outline; 2-pixel pale upper-left rim. Restrained lime-white #E9FFB8 eye edged pale lime; few tiny leaf-green vein accents; vivid green occupies less than 12 percent of each body. Fine crisp high-detail game pixel art comparable to premium painted space shooter enemy sprites, clear silhouette at phone scale. Background MUST be genuinely transparent alpha. No labels, text, scenery, ground, shadows, frames, other creatures, glow clouds, slime smears, red, pink, magenta, violet, cyan, teal fields, orange or amber fields. Keep all three centered and complete, nothing cropped.
