Both enemies must clearly read as FLYING aircraft/gunships (swept wings or fins, visible engine nozzles with small orange flames at the rear/top, no legs, no claws touching ground, no crab/insect look). Keep the same names and roles.

COLOR: do NOT make them one orange color. Use several distinct saturated accent colors per craft: e.g. deep teal / cobalt blue / violet hull plating, bright gold or brass trim, hot orange-white engine and weapon glow, a cool cyan lens or canopy. Colors must be far from player red and no pink/magenta anywhere.
