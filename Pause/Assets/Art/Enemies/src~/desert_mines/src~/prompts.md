# Mirage Mine image generation prompts

Each candidate was generated separately with built-in image generation and `transparent_background=true`. All are source paintings for the Dormant pose. Candidate 1 was selected after visual inspection because its compact body, left clamp, and lens center best match the Tide row.

## Candidate 1 — selected

> Use case: game sprite concept. One complete isolated DORMANT Mirage Mine for Pause, a vertical 2D shooter. Orthographic top-down view, rugged highly detailed neon pixel art. A circular dark rusted brass-copper lens sphere, 4 slightly asymmetric worn brass retaining claws around it, sand dust crusting its upper arc, dark almost black central glass iris; heavy rectangular industrial clamp extends left from the body and visibly grips an unseen vertical rail. Approximate overall silhouette: body round 65% of width, clamp another 25%, centered vertically. Dense small pixel clusters, 1-pixel dark indigo outline, 4-6 tonal ramps, tiny bolts, scratches, chipped paint, directional pale-bone upper-left rim. Dormant is matte and unlit. Transparent background, no rail, no floor, one object only, no text. Palette: desaturated bone sand hue 40-48 S<=.33, dark matte oxidized copper hue 22-30 S<=.62, subtle dusty indigo shadows. No glow, no orange, no red, no pink, no violet, no saturated cyan or green.
