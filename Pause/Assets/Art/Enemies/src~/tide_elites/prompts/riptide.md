Ship: RIPTIDE, a surf-dash lancer, a long lean ram-fighter. A needle-nose armoured prow ending in a hardened lance spike with a small amber emitter tip; a narrow segmented fuselage spine of layered armour plates; two long swept-back blade wings that are heavy armoured airfoil slabs on hardpoints (panel seams, bolted leading-edge guards, small control flaps), not fins; a raised dorsal intake spine; TWO large engine nozzles at the back, set close together.
PALETTE (invented for this ship): ultramarine / cobalt blue painted armour (#2450D8 mid, #16307A shadow plates), polished silver-white leading-edge guards and spine plates (#DDE8F5), brushed gunmetal frame, ONE accent: bright amber (#FFB020) on the lance tip, wing-tip lamps and thin intake slits; tiny cyan-white status LEDs. Silver trim, not brass.
