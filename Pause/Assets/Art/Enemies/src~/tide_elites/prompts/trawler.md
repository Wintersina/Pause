Ship: TRAWLER, a net-hauling armoured strike barge, wide and blunt. A broad flat-fronted armoured hull with a raised bridge block at the front, two long outrigger net-boom arms reaching out to both sides (lattice booms with pulleys, cable runs and heavy cable spools at their tips), a central winch drum with wound steel cable on the rear deck, a bow shovel-plate; a row of cargo hatches; two big engine nozzles at the back.
PALETTE (invented for this ship): weathered safety-tangerine orange painted hull plates (#EE7A24 mid, #A24510 shadow, #FFB06A worn highlights), charcoal-graphite deck and frame (#2C3138), pale cream-white bridge block and hatch edges (#F0E6CE), cold steel-blue booms and winch cable (#6F8FB5), ONE glow: warm yellow bridge windows and amber hazard lamps (#FFD21F). Orange is dominant; keep the stripes minimal and structural, never decorative banding.
