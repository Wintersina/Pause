Ship: BATHYSCAPHE, a depth-charge siege platform, a heavy armoured gunship built around a riveted spherical pressure hull. A big round brass pressure sphere at the centre (riveted bands, a reinforced equatorial ring, three small round viewports lit warm white), held in a dark angular steel cradle frame; two heavy articulated grapple-claw arms with hydraulic pistons reaching forward-outward from the cradle shoulders, each ending in a rugged clamp that carries a charge; a depth-charge rack with 3 canisters slung across the lower front of the sphere; short stub lift-wings on the cradle; two large engine nozzles at the back flanking the rack.
PALETTE (invented for this ship): burnished brass-gold sphere (#D9A441 mid, #8A5A1C shadow, #F3D58A highlights), deep indigo-navy steel cradle and claws (#26305E / #161C3A), pale bone-grey piston rods and clamp teeth, ONE glow family: warm amber-white lamps in the viewports and on the charge canisters (#FFD27A), plus a few tiny aquamarine (#5BE6D2) status lights.
