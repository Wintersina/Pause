Ship: GLASS CANNON, a flying siege-artillery platform (it was a buried silo cannon, now an airborne gun deck). A broad, low-slung armoured shoulder deck with buttressed side pods, and ONE huge central cannon barrel pointing up along the nose line, ending in a muzzle brake; at the barrel's root a big fused-glass sun-lens in a heavy mount (faceted glass with a hot focus point deep inside), smaller secondary lens turrets on each pod, recoil pistons and ammo feed, the hull tapering to the rear with 2 big engine bells. Heavy, serious, precise.
PALETTE (invented for this ship): graphite-black and brushed titanium armour with cool slate highlights; the lens glass in amber-gold glowing to a white-hot core (the only warm area); muted steel-teal inset glass slits and small teal lamps.
