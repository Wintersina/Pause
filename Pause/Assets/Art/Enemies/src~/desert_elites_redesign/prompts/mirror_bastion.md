Ship: MIRROR BASTION, a heavy shielded warship. A wide, broad, heavily armoured hull ringed in overlapping, shingled mirror-glass shield plates (big layered curved plates like a pangolin's or a tortoise's armour, with a clear gap in the middle of the front row), a squat armoured command block on the spine, a central lens cannon at the front, two flanking cap-turrets on the shoulders, thick buttresses, 3 big engine bells at the stern. Immovable, heavy, serious: an armoured citadel in the air.
PALETTE (invented for this ship): deep aubergine / plum-violet armour plates with a dark ink-violet underlayer; the shingled shield plates in silvered mirror glass with ice-cyan reflections and dark edge shading; burnished gold trim and bolts; a small pale-gold core lamp.
