Ship: DUNE STALKER, a burrowing-shark interceptor reborn as a flying lance fighter. A long, lean wedge hull: a hardened drill-lance nose spike (brass-tipped, segmented, like a shark's rostrum), shark-scute armour plates running down the spine, two swept dorsal blade-fins on armoured hardpoints, small grit-needle ports along both flanks, a tapering segmented keel to the rear with 2 engine bells. The lean, predatory build of a hunter, aggressive but heavy hardware, not a toy.
PALETTE (invented for this ship): deep cobalt / midnight-blue armour with a lighter steel-blue highlight ramp; pale bone-ivory ceramic plates over the spine and fin roots; brass lance tip and brass fittings; small gold lamps. Cool and crisp against the warm sand.
