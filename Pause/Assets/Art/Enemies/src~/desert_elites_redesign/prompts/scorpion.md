Ship: SCORPION SKIFF (formerly the Scorpion Walker, now a flying design with NO LEGS). An armoured venom-strike gunship shaped like a scorpion seen from above: a segmented armoured carapace body of overlapping plates, two big mechanical pincer-arms swept forward on armoured hardpoints (articulated, riveted, each ending in a heavy claw), and the long segmented tail-boom curling up and over the back ending in a stinger cannon with a glowing tip (drawn clearly, symmetric, rising straight along the centre line); instead of legs, short stub-wing pylons each side carrying ducted thrusters; 2 engine bells at the stern. Menacing heavy hardware.
PALETTE (invented for this ship): dark burnt-sienna / umber lacquered armour plates over black gunmetal steel; polished gold-white claw tips and a white-gold glowing stinger tip; thin amber-gold glow seams between carapace segments. Warm and dark: value contrast against pale sand comes from the deep plates.
