Ship: SCORPION SKIFF (formerly the Scorpion Walker, now a flying design with NO LEGS). An armoured venom-strike gunship shaped like a scorpion seen from above: a segmented armoured carapace body of overlapping plates, two big mechanical pincer-arms swept forward on armoured hardpoints (articulated, riveted, each ending in a heavy claw), and the long segmented tail-boom rising straight up the centre line over the body ending in a stinger cannon with a glowing tip (drawn clearly, symmetric); instead of legs, short stub-wing pylons each side carrying ducted thrusters; 2 engine bells at the stern. Menacing heavy hardware.
PALETTE (invented for this ship, IMPORTANT, NOT RED): dark bronzed umber-brown and burnt-bronze armour plates (orange-brown hue, like old oiled bronze or dark chocolate-bronze lacquer; clearly NOT red, NOT brick, NOT crimson, NOT rust-red) over black gunmetal steel; polished pale-gold claw tips and a white-gold glowing stinger tip; thin amber-gold glow seams between carapace segments. Deep value contrast against pale sand comes from the dark brown plates.
