Ship: LENS MATRIARCH, a slim mirror-array heliograph skirmisher. A narrow elegant armoured spine/fuselage with a sensor spike nose, and a symmetrical fan of SIX separate mirror panels (three per side) on hinged brass articulated arms. The panels are NOT leaf or petal shapes (avoid any plant or leaf look): they are faceted angular reflector plates, like hexagonal / kite-shaped heliograph mirror segments with a thick riveted frame and a slight dish curve, swept forward and outward in a tight crescent so the whole ship is about as wide as 70% of its length, not wider. A small hot core lens at the centre of the spine, thin rod emitters at the panel roots, 2 slim engine bells at the stern. Delicate-but-military, a precision optical instrument.
The mirror surfaces must be drawn as pixel art with flat banded facets: a simple sky gradient from pale cyan to deep blue with one crisp white sun glint and dark steel edge shading. NO photographic reflection, no landscape, no horizon, no clouds, no trees.
PALETTE (invented for this ship): deep emerald / malachite-green armoured spine with dark green-black recesses; the mirror plates polished steel-chrome with the cyan-blue sky tint; brass hinges and arm joints; white-gold core lens.
