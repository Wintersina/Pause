Ship: CARAVAN HAULER, a flying cargo lifter (it was a land crawler-barge, now it hauls on the air). A wide, flat, heavy-lift freighter: an armoured structural spine and cargo cradle carrying THREE big cylindrical sand-pit canisters slung side by side (ribbed steel tanks with valve wheels, hazard hatches and clamps, clearly separate cylinders), a blunt armoured cab at the front with a slit canopy, heavy outrigger lift pylons with big ducted fans on both sides, riveted deck plating and cargo straps, 3 large engine bells across the stern. It looks like a rugged industrial truck of the sky, with real weight.
PALETTE (invented for this ship): charcoal-graphite structural frame and cab; the canisters in saffron-yellow painted steel with dark steel bands and worn chipped paint; turquoise running lights and canopy glass; a touch of brass on valves.
