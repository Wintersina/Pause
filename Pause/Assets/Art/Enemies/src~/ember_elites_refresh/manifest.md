Cells: 0–3 idle flight; 4 tell; 5 action; 6 hit/damaged. Source paintings: three concepts per ship in `src~/`; selected panel index below is zero-based. Built-in image generation supplied all painted hull content; this script crops and scales with nearest-neighbour sampling, cleans forbidden hues, adds the pale rim and small state details.
